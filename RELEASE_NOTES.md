# v0.3.0-pre — macOS 27.2.x / arm64

Online installer for Athena, Artemis, Hephaestus and Stand-alone Atoms.

## Assets

- `Demeter-macOS-27.2-online-arm64-v0.3.0-pre.dmg`
- `compatibility-source-v0.3.0-pre.zip`
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`

## Included

- Private MacPorts, compiler, Ifeffit, Perl/Wx/PDL, Demeter and plotting dependencies.
- Four native app launchers, Light/Dark appearance and layout fixes.
- Cocoa gradient crash and Athena data-import fixes.
- Athena gray zoom-box, coordinate markers and foreground menu bridge.
- Complete compatibility sources, rebuild scripts and license notices.

**Requires:** macOS 27.2.x, arm64, full Xcode, internet, at least 30 GiB free space and an ASCII home path without spaces.

**Pre-release:** first build may take hours. Ad hoc signed, not notarized. Clean-machine installation and recent plot mouse/menu GUI acceptance remain unverified.
